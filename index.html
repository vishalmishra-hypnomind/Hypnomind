HypnoMind is an educational platform focused on clinical hypnotherapy concepts, subconscious-mind education, communication, mentalism and ethical mind-reading style demonstrations.

What you can learn

A clear learning path designed for students, aspiring practitioners and people interested in the science, practice and performance side of the mind.

  • Foundations of hypnosis, suggestion and focused attention
  • Session structure, communication and rapport skills
  • Relaxation and guided-imagery techniques
  • Subconscious-mind concepts and habit-focused work
  • Observation, body-language and conversational cues
  • Mentalism and “mind reading” performance techniques
  • Ethics, consent, boundaries and responsible practice
  • Recorded lessons plus selected live interactive sessions
